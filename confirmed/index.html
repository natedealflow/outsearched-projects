Questions buyers ask

Straight answers before you meet

The same answers you’ll hear on the call, so you can spend the time on your search.

What happens on the call?

About 30 minutes with Nate. We pressure-test your buy box, size how many owners fit it, and show you how we would reach them. If there is a fit, we map your first 90 days.

What does OutSearched cost?

There is one buy-side engagement: a one-time $2,000 setup fee, $5,000 per month, and 1.5% of transaction value at close with a $75,000 minimum. The initial term is six months, then month to month with 30 days written notice.

How is this different from a broker?

A broker works for the seller and runs a process with competing buyers. We work for you, the buyer, and reach owners who are not for sale yet, so you are not bidding against a room.

Who do you work with?

Independent sponsors, search funds, family offices, lower middle market private equity and corporate development teams with a defined buy box and capital lined up or in process.

How fast will I see deals?

Every search is different, and no timeline is promised. Several clients report quality deals or an LOI within their first 60 days; you can read their reviews on Trustpilot.

Do you guarantee a closed deal?

No. Nobody can honestly promise a close. You get weekly pipeline reporting and a strategy call every two weeks, so you always see the work and the results.

Is my search confidential?

Yes. Both sides keep each other’s non-public information confidential, during and after the engagement.

What if I need to reschedule?

Use the reschedule link in your confirmation email. It keeps your spot with Nate and frees the time for another buyer.
